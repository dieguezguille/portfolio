## Context

[Ruvibet](https://en.ruvibet.com) is the game studio of [OneBitTech](https://www.onebittech.com/en), an iGaming company from Tucumán, Argentina, that builds a [casino platform](https://www.onebittech.com/en/casino) for licensed operators. The studio has its own art, math and development teams. From October 2020 to December 2021 I owned the frontend of its games in Unity and C#, working closely with the backend and UI/UX teams.

## Games

Both are avalanche games: winning symbols clear and new ones fall into their place, with multipliers and a maximum win of 2,000 times the bet. Both are available in Spanish, Portuguese and English.

- **[Aymara](https://en.ruvibet.com/game/aymara-en/)**: a jungle adventure, for the browser. We built it as a team. [Play the demo](https://en.ruvibet.com/game/aymara-en/demo/).
- **[Aliens and Fruits](https://en.ruvibet.com/game/aliens-and-fruits-en/)**: fruits against alien invaders, for the browser and mobile. I refactored it and fixed bugs. [Play the demo](https://en.ruvibet.com/game/aliens-and-fruits-en/demo/).

In both I refactored legacy code.

## Team

I created the training program for new team members, who became long-term contributors to the product, and introduced unit testing, pair programming and mob programming.

## Outcome

- **Shipped**: both games are in Ruvibet's catalog for operators, played by thousands, and their demos are public.
